Mero Kaagaj Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version

  • Your documents, photos, signatures and invoices are processed on your phone. We never upload them, and we have no server that receives them.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Who we are

Mero Kaagaj ("the app") is published by [DEVELOPER NAME]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Files you work with

When you scan a page, pick a photo or file, resize a photo or signature, create a PDF or invoice, or open a document, all processing happens on your device. Results are saved where you choose (for example Documents/MeroKaagaj or Pictures/MeroKaagaj) and shared only when you tap Share. We do not collect, store or see these files.

Your invoice sender details, currency and tax settings, and saved size presets are stored only on your phone and are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Camera

The camera is used only while you scan a document, photo or signature. Images stay on your phone.

Google services used inside the app

  • Google ML Kit (document scanner, background removal) and the Google code scanner (QR and barcodes) run on your device through Google Play services. Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage information about these features to keep them working. See ML Kit terms.
  • Google AdMob shows ads. To do this, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions with ads, and crash and performance data. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Learn how Google uses data from partner apps.

Ads and your choices

  • No ads are shown the first time you use the app.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change this any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

Size presets

The app may download an updated list of upload-size presets (for example passport photo sizes) from a public web page. This download contains no personal data. As with any web request, the host may log your IP address.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data sent by Google's services is encrypted in transit. Files you protect with a password in the app are encrypted on your device.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your files or any account data, there is nothing for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app stores, clear the app's data or uninstall it. For data held by Google, use the links above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
